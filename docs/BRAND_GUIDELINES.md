# Brand Guidelines (working draft)

- Official name: **NEXT | HR & Recruitment** (English form kept in all locales; Arabic descriptive text may sit beside it).
- Slogan: **Your next step starts here** / Arabic: خطوتك القادمة تبدأ من هنا.
- Location: Karrada, Baghdad, Iraq. Primary market Iraq; languages Arabic + English (RTL/LTR).

## Logo
- Source: the user-supplied original files, used **unmodified**: `public/brand/next-logo-full.png` (with tagline) and `public/brand/next-mark.png` (compact mark). Components: `src/components/brand/Logo.tsx`.
- Both are raster PNGs. **An approved SVG / high-resolution original is still requested** for email templates, PDFs and favicons at all sizes. The logo has not been redrawn or altered.
- Do not stretch, recolor, crop the frame, or hide the tagline in the full variant. Use the mark only where the full logo does not fit.
- The advertisement/banner image supplied is reference only, not a logo.

## Colors — PROVISIONAL (not official codes)
Dark Blue #124B89 · Corporate Blue #1769B4 · Light Blue #65B9F2 · Soft Blue #EAF5FD · Dark Text #172536 · Gray Text #66758A · Soft Background #F7FAFD · White. Defined as tokens in `src/app/globals.css`; replace with values sampled from the approved logo file.

## Typography
Inter (Latin) and IBM Plex Sans Arabic (Arabic), self-hosted via @fontsource (Google Fonts is unreachable at build time in some environments).

## Content rules
Mission/Vision are *proposed* texts pending company approval. No invented statistics, clients, testimonials or follow counts. Contact phone/email and Instagram/LinkedIn URLs stay empty until supplied and verified (to be set from the Admin dashboard); numbers visible inside ad images are not company contact data.
